Trust doesn't collect payments.

Most shops selling phones on installment have one enforcement tool: asking nicely, then threatening repossession. LockPilot gives you a system instead — the phone enforces the agreement the customer already signed.

01

Provision at sale

Scan a QR code before handing the phone over. LockPilot registers as the device's owner and links it to the customer's installment plan — takes under a minute at the counter.

02

Track every rupee

Installment schedule, payments, and running balance live in your dashboard. Record a payment on the spot and the customer's balance updates immediately.

03

Enforcement runs itself

Miss a payment past the grace period, the phone locks on its own. Pay, and it unlocks the same second — no phone calls, no chasing.

Built for how a shop actually runs.

Uninstall & reset protection

A customer can't remove the app or wipe the phone while installments are outstanding. Both restrictions lift automatically the moment the phone is paid off.

Grace-period locking

Set your own grace period per shop. Customers get reminders before anything locks — enforcement is the last step, not the first.

Full audit trail

Every lock, unlock, and payment is logged with who recorded it and when — so a dispute is a five-second lookup, not a guessing game.

Manual override, always

A prominent unlock button sits in your dashboard at all times. Automatic enforcement never leaves you without a way to fix a mistake.

Per-device profit tracking

Cost price against sale price, per device and in aggregate — you always know your margin, not just your revenue.

Multi-branch ready

Run more than one location under a single account, with staff permissions scoped to each branch.

Built to be seen, not hidden.

A lock screen that hides who's behind it feels like spyware. One that names the shop and gives a number to call feels like an overdue bill — because that's what it is.
